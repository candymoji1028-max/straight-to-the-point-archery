Welcome to Straight to the Point Archery

Straight to the Point Archery offers classes for all skill levels, from beginners to advanced students. Our certified instructors help students build confidence, technique, and safety skills in a supportive environment.

We have excellent network of contacts with the USA Archery (USAA), Washington State Archery Association (WSAA), Washington State Bow Hunters (WSB), National Field Archery Association (NFAA) and many more national and state archery associations. These partnerships allow you to feel confident in our facility and staff.

Beginner Classes

Perfect for newcomers of all ages. Students learn safety, basic form, and foundational shooting skills in a supportive environment.

Intermediate Classes

For students who understand the basics and want to improve accuracy, consistency, and technique through guided practice.

Advanced Classes

Designed for experienced archers looking to refine precision, build strength, and train with more advanced drills.
